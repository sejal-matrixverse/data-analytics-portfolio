# Regional & Segment Analysis

## Objective

The regional and segment analysis evaluates sales and profitability across geographic regions and customer segments.

## Key Metrics

- Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Customer Segment Performance
- Regional Performance

## Analysis Performed

The analysis covers:

- Net Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Profit Margin by Region
- Sales by Customer Segment
- Profit by Customer Segment
- Profit Margin by Customer Segment
- Region × Customer Segment Performance

## Key Insights

- Regional performance varies across sales, profit, and profit margins.
- The West region is a strong contributor to overall sales and profit.
- The South region demonstrates strong profitability relative to its sales contribution.
- Consumer customers contribute significantly to overall sales.
- Customer segments differ in profitability and should be evaluated beyond sales volume alone.

## Business Recommendations

- Prioritise high-performing regions while investigating opportunities in weaker markets.
- Develop region-specific sales strategies based on profitability.
- Focus customer retention and growth strategies on high-value segments.
- Evaluate regional and segment performance together to identify the most profitable business opportunities.
