# Profitability Analysis

## Objective

The profitability analysis evaluates the relationship between sales, costs, discounts, and profit to identify the key drivers of business profitability.

## Key Metrics

- Net Sales
- Total Cost
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Discount Amount
- Discount Percentage

## Analysis Performed

The analysis covers:

- Profit by Category
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Profit by Region
- Profit by Customer Segment
- Top 10 Most Profitable Products
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit
- Discount vs Profitability

## Key Insights

- Profitability varies significantly across categories, sub-categories, regions, and products.
- High sales do not necessarily translate into high profit.
- Excessive discounting can reduce profit margins.
- Certain products and sub-categories require closer monitoring due to low or negative profitability.
- Profit margin provides additional insight beyond total sales when evaluating business performance.

## Business Recommendations

- Focus on products and categories with strong profit margins.
- Review discount strategies for low-margin products.
- Analyse cost structures for products with negative profitability.
- Use profit margin alongside sales when evaluating product and regional performance.
- Regularly monitor discount levels and their impact on profitability.
