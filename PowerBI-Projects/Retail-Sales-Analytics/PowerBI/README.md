# Retail Sales Analytics – Power BI Dashboard

## Overview

This Power BI dashboard provides an interactive analysis of retail sales performance, profitability, product performance, and regional and customer segment trends.

The dashboard connects the analytical results from PostgreSQL and SQL with interactive Power BI visualisations to support data-driven business decision-making.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level view of business performance through:

- Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Orders
- Month-over-Month Sales Growth
- Monthly Net Sales Trend
- Net Sales by Category
- Net Sales by Region
- Top 10 Products

### 2. Profitability Analysis

Focuses on understanding the key drivers of profitability through:

- Profit by Category
- Profit by Region
- Profit Margin by Category
- Profit by Sub-Category
- Top 10 Most Profitable Products
- Bottom 10 Products by Profit
- Discount vs Profit Analysis

### 3. Product Performance

Evaluates product-level performance using:

- Product and Category Matrix
- Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Top 10 Products by Net Sales
- Top 10 Products by Profit
- Sales vs Profitability Scatter Plot

### 4. Regional & Segment Analysis

Evaluates geographic and customer segment performance through:

- Net Sales by Region
- Profit by Region
- Profit Margin by Customer Segment
- Sales and Profit by Customer Segment
- Regional Sales vs Profitability
- Region and Segment Performance Matrix

## Key Features

- Interactive slicers and filters
- KPI cards
- Drill-down analysis
- Conditional formatting
- Data bars
- Top 10 product analysis
- Profitability analysis
- Interactive cross-filtering
- DAX-based measures
- SQL-based calculated metrics

## Tools & Technologies

- Power BI Desktop
- DAX
- PostgreSQL
- SQL

## Business Value

The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor sales and profitability, identify high-performing products and regions, evaluate customer segments, and identify areas requiring pricing, discount, or product-level optimisation.
