# Regional & Segment Analysis

## Objective

This dashboard analyses regional and customer segment performance
to identify differences in sales, profitability, and profit margins.

## KPIs

- Total Net Sales
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Orders

## Analysis Performed

1. Profit by Region
2. Net Sales by Region
3. Sales & Profit by Customer Segment
4. Profit Margin by Customer Segment
5. Region × Segment Performance
6. Regional Sales vs Profitability

## Business Questions

- Which region generates the highest sales?
- Which region generates the highest profit?
- Which customer segment generates the highest revenue?
- Which customer segment has the highest profit margin?
- Are high-sales regions also highly profitable?
- Which region-segment combinations require further investigation?
