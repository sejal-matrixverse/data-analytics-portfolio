# Business Requirements

## Project Overview

The Retail Sales Analytics project aims to analyse retail sales data and provide actionable insights into sales performance, profitability, product performance, and regional and customer segment trends.

## Business Objectives

- Analyse overall sales and profit performance.
- Track key business KPIs such as Net Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, Total Orders, and Total Quantity.
- Identify the highest and lowest-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Analyse regional and customer segment performance.
- Identify the top-performing and least-profitable products.
- Evaluate the impact of discounts on profitability.
- Enable interactive analysis using Power BI dashboards.

## Key Business Questions

1. What is the overall sales and profitability performance?
2. Which categories and sub-categories generate the highest profit?
3. Which products contribute most to sales and profit?
4. Which regions and customer segments perform best?
5. Which products generate low or negative profit?
6. How do discounts affect profitability?
7. What areas require business attention or improvement?

## Expected Outcome

The project provides an end-to-end analytical solution using PostgreSQL, SQL, and Power BI to transform raw retail transaction data into meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.
