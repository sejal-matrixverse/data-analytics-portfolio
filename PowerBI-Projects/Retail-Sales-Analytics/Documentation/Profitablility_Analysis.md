# Profitability Analysis

## Objective

The Profitability Analysis dashboard evaluates profitability across
categories, sub-categories, products, and regions.

## Key KPIs

- Total Profit
- Profit Margin %
- Total Cost
- Total Discount

## Analysis Performed

1. Profit by Category
2. Profit by Sub-Category
3. Profit Margin % by Category
4. Top 10 Most Profitable Products
5. Bottom 10 Products by Profit
6. Discount vs Profit Analysis
7. Profit by Region

## Business Questions

- Which categories generate the highest profit?
- Which sub-categories are most profitable?
- Which categories have the strongest profit margins?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which products have the lowest or negative profitability?
- Does discounting impact profit?
- Which regions generate the highest profit?
