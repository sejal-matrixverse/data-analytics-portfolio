# Customer Risk Analysis

## Objective

Analyze customer creditworthiness and identify potential loan default risks.

---

## Metrics Used

- Credit Score
- Loan Amount
- Annual Income
- Default Status
- Loan Type
- Region
- Employment Type

---

## Risk Indicators

### High Risk

- Low credit score
- High loan amount
- Existing default history

### Medium Risk

- Moderate credit score
- Average income
- Medium loan amount

### Low Risk

- High credit score
- Stable income
- No default history

---

## Business Questions

- Which customers are likely to default?
- Which loan types carry the highest risk?
- Which regions experience more defaults?
- Does credit score affect approval?
- How does income relate to loan amount?

---

## Key Insights

- Higher credit scores generally correspond to lower default risk.
- Business and Home loans contribute the largest portfolio share.
- Default rate remains low across the portfolio.
- Regional loan performance varies significantly.
- Customer income positively influences approved loan amounts.
