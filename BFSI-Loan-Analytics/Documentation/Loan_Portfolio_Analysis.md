# Loan Portfolio Analysis

## Objective

Evaluate the overall health and performance of the bank's loan portfolio.

---

## Analysis Performed

### Portfolio Summary

- Total Applications
- Total Loan Amount
- Total Interest
- Approval Rate
- Default Rate

---

### Loan Type Analysis

Compared loan portfolio across:

- Business
- Home
- Education
- Personal
- Auto

---

### Regional Analysis

Compared:

- East
- West
- North
- South

Measured:

- Loan Amount
- Applications
- Approval Rate

---

### Customer Analysis

Studied:

- Credit Score
- Employment Type
- Income
- Gender
- Age Group

---

### Risk Analysis

Measured:

- Default Rate
- Loan Defaults
- Credit Score Distribution
- High Risk Customers

---

## Business Outcome

The analysis provides a comprehensive overview of lending operations, customer behavior, and portfolio risk, enabling management to optimize lending strategies and monitor business performance.
