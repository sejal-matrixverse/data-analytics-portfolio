# Business Requirements

## Project Name

BFSI Loan Portfolio Analytics

---

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to build an interactive Business Intelligence solution that enables bank executives to monitor loan portfolio performance, customer demographics, regional operations, and portfolio risk for informed decision-making.

---

## Business Goals

- Monitor loan applications and approvals.
- Track total loan disbursement.
- Analyze approval and rejection trends.
- Identify default risk across customer segments.
- Evaluate regional and branch performance.
- Understand customer demographics.
- Improve lending decisions using analytics.

---

## Stakeholders

- Executive Management
- Branch Managers
- Credit Risk Team
- Loan Operations Team
- Business Analysts

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Applications
- Approved Loans
- Rejected Loans
- Pending Loans
- Total Loan Amount
- Total Interest
- Approval Rate
- Default Rate
- Average Loan Amount
- Average Credit Score
- Average Customer Income
